About News Desk

Powered by:
gateway.warerastats.io by Hattorius
api2.warera.io
api5.warera.io

The War Era News Desk was created to support players who take on the role of journalists, providing a centralized workspace to monitor, organize, and collect information from every significant event unfolding across the world of War Era.

Its primary purpose is to enhance the journalistic experience by making information easier to discover and analyze while preserving the core principles of journalism: credibility, transparency, and accuracy.

The project is developed as Free and Open Source Software (FOSS), allowing anyone to inspect the source code, contribute to its development, or adapt it for their own needs. The source repository is available at:

https://github.com/exwayz/war-era-news-desk

From its very beginning, War Era News Desk has embraced the KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid) philosophy. Every feature is designed with practicality in mind, favoring clarity, efficiency, and reliability over unnecessary visual complexity. The goal is not to build the most elaborate interface, but to create a dependable newsroom tool that helps journalists focus on what matters most: the story.

Typewriter Help

How to write, format and publish a War Era article
Quick start

Typewriter is a focused rich text editor built to match the War Era article editor. You type a headline, write the body, format and style the text, save your work as a draft, then copy the finished HTML and paste it into the War Era editor.

The full workflow

1. Type the headline in the title box above the editor. The title is saved automatically and stays with your draft.

2. Write in the large editor area. Everything you type is saved locally in your browser and restored the next time you open News Desk.

3. Select text and shape it with the toolbar above the editor, or use the keyboard shortcuts listed below.

4. Press the Save draft button in the panel header at any time to store the current work in the Draft Library on the right.

5. When the article is finished, press the HTML button to copy the TipTap HTML to your clipboard.

6. Press the external link button to open the War Era editor in a new tab, paste the copied HTML into the article body, and publish.

Toolbar

The toolbar holds every formatting and insert tool, grouped by purpose. Select text first, then press a button, or apply the tool to the paragraph the caret is in.

Block type

The select on the left switches the current block between P, H1, H2 and H3. Put the caret in a paragraph, pick a type, and the block becomes a heading or returns to a paragraph.

Text formatting

Bold thickens the selection, Italic slants it, Underline underlines it and Strikethrough draws a line through it. Ctrl+B Ctrl+I Ctrl+U

Inline code renders the selection in a monospace font, perfect for names, IDs and technical words.

Font and color

The font select changes the family of the selected text and matches the fonts the War Era editor understands. The color select applies a War Era themed text color to the selection.

Alignment

Align the current block left, center, right or justified. These cover the paragraph, headings and quotes.

Ruler, lists and indentation

Horizontal ruler inserts a divider line. Typing --- on its own line then pressing Enter does the same thing.

Numbered list and Bulleted list convert the current paragraphs into a list. Type 1. then a space to start a numbered list, or - then a space for a bulleted list.

Indent and Outdent move a line or a nested list item in and out. Tab indents, Shift+Tab outdents.

Quote, code and collapsible

Quote block styles the paragraph as an editorial quote. Code block wraps it in a monospace, preformatted block. Collapsible section inserts an expandable details block that the War Era editor renders with a built in toggle.

Insert

Link asks for a URL and wraps the selection in a hyperlink. Image asks for an image URL and inserts the image. YouTube and TikTok embed a video from its share link, ready for the War Era player.

The editor

The large area is a full rich text surface. Most formatting is inherited from the toolbar, and a few behaviors are built in.

Mentions and locked chips

Type @ anywhere to open the mention picker. Search any user, country, region or faction, then pick one to insert it as a locked newsroom chip. Chips are protected: formatting and even backspace never damage them, the whole chip is deleted at once, and the caret snaps safely around it.

Smart typing

1. then space starts a numbered list. - then space starts a bulleted list. --- then Enter inserts a horizontal rule. Enter inside the summary of a collapsible section moves the caret into the collapsible body.

Live status

The counter bar under the editor shows the character count, the word count, the paragraph count (one line is one block) and the caret position as line, column and character offset. It updates while you type.

Spellcheck

Switch spellcheck on from the sidebar. It marks misspelled words in the whole document, including text that was already written, not just new words.

Zoom

Hold Ctrl and roll the mouse wheel to scale the text. The zoom level is remembered between sessions.

Sidebar

The narrow strip on the right of the editor holds the editor tools, from top to bottom.

Zoom in and Zoom out scale the text in 5% steps. The percentage button in the middle resets the zoom to 100%.

Undo reverts the last action and Redo replays it. Each button is grayed out when there is nothing to undo or redo. Ctrl+Z Ctrl+Y

Find and replace opens the search box. Type a term, read the live match count, and press Next to jump between matches. Tick the Replace checkbox to reveal the replace field and the Replace and Replace all buttons. Esc closes the search at any time.

Spellcheck toggles the browser spell checker for the whole document.

Clear wipes the editor back to a single empty paragraph. The cleared state is saved, so reloading keeps it clean.

Save, Copy HTML and Open War Era editor

Three buttons sit at the top of the Typewriter panel and cover the whole export path.

Save draft stores the current title and body in the Draft Library, stamped with the date and time. Nothing leaves your browser.

Copy HTML serializes the article into TipTap compatible HTML, exactly the format the War Era editor reads, cleans up loose markup, and puts it on your clipboard. A toast confirms the copy.

Open War Era editor opens the article editor at app.warera.io in a new tab. From there, paste the copied HTML into the article body and continue with the official War Era tools: thumbnails, categories and publishing.

Draft Library

The library lives on the right side of the panel, above the images. Every saved draft is a card showing the title and the date and time it was last saved. When the library is empty a hint tells you to write something and press Save draft.

Click a card to load that draft into the editor. The card you are working on is highlighted as current.

Each card has three action buttons:

Save to this draft takes the current editor state and overwrites that draft, refreshing its timestamp. Use it to update an older draft with newer changes.

Rename swaps the card for a small input so you can change the title without touching the content. Enter saves, Esc cancels.

Delete removes the draft immediately. Deleting has no undo.

Image Library

The image library reuses the same images across articles instead of hunting URLs every time. It accepts direct image links from imgur, giphy and tenor.

Paste a direct image link into the small input and press the add button. Duplicates are ignored, and a toast confirms the image was added.

Thumbnails appear in the grid below. Click any thumbnail to insert that image into the article at the caret. The small mark on a thumbnail removes the image from the library only, it never touches the article.

- + spaceStart a bulleted list
--- + EnterInsert a horizontal rule
EscClose the search box and popups

Notes

Your work stays private. The title, the article body, the drafts and the image library are stored only in your browser, in local storage. Nothing is transmitted until you open the War Era editor and paste the copy there yourself.

Typewriter output is plain TipTap HTML. The War Era editor accepts it as is, without extra conversion steps.

Protected content, such as mention chips, is never altered by formatting and survives saving, loading, copy and pasting.

The project is open source: inspect it and report issues at github.com/exwayz/war-era-news-desk.
